### **C. Numerical Types (12 Distinct Types)**

1. **Charge from Current and Time:**  
   **Example:** A current of 2A flows for 5 seconds. Find the charge.  
   **Method A:** Q = I × t = 2 × 5 = 10 C.  
   **Method B:** Direct multiplication.  
   **Answer:** 10 C.  

2. **Number of Electrons from Charge:**  
   **Example:** Find the number of electrons in 10 C of charge.  
   **Method A:** n = Q/e = 10/(1.6 × 10^-19) = 6.25 × 10^19.  
   **Method B:** Direct division.  
   **Answer:** 6.25 × 10^19 electrons.  

3. **Work from V and Q:**  
   **Example:** A charge of 10 C moves through a potential difference of 6V. Find the work done.  
   **Method A:** W = V × Q = 6 × 10 = 60 J.  
   **Method B:** Direct multiplication.  
   **Answer:** 60 J.  

4. **Resistance from Resistivity:**  
   **Example:** A copper wire (ρ = 1.7 × 10^-8 Ωm) has length 2m and area 1 × 10^-6 m². Find its resistance.  
   **Method A:** R = ρL/A = (1.7 × 10^-8 × 2)/(1 × 10^-6) = 0.034 Ω.  
   **Method B:** Direct substitution.  
   **Answer:** 0.034 Ω.  

5. **Resistivity from Data:**  
   **Example:** A wire of length 3m, area 2 × 10^-6 m², and resistance 0.03 Ω. Find its resistivity.  
   **Method A:** ρ = RA/L = (0.03 × 2 × 10^-6)/3 = 2 × 10^-8 Ωm.  
   **Method B:** Direct substitution.  
   **Answer:** 2 × 10^-8 Ωm.  

6. **Stretched/Folded Wire:**  
   **Example:** A wire of resistance 4Ω is stretched to double its length. Find its new resistance.  
   **Method A:** R' = 4 × (2)^2 = 16 Ω.  
   **Method B:** Direct proportionality.  
   **Answer:** 16 Ω.  

7. **Series Resistance:**  
   **Example:** Three resistors (2Ω, 3Ω, 5Ω) are connected in series. Find the total resistance.  
   **Method A:** R = R1 + R2 + R3 = 2 + 3 + 5 = 10 Ω.  
   **Method B:** Direct addition.  
   **Answer:** 10 Ω.  

8. **Parallel Resistance:**  
   **Example:** Three resistors (2Ω, 4Ω, 8Ω) are connected in parallel. Find the total resistance.  
   **Method A:** 1/R = 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 = 7/8 ⇒ R = 8/7 Ω.  
   **Method B:** Direct addition of reciprocals.  
   **Answer:** 8/7 Ω.  

9. **Mixed Series-Parallel:**  
   **Example:** A 2Ω resistor is connected in series with a parallel combination of 4Ω and 8Ω. Find the total resistance.  
   **Method A:** R_parallel = 1/(1/4 + 1/8) = 8/3 Ω; R_total = 2 + 8/3 = 14/3 Ω.  
   **Method B:** Step-by-step calculation.  
   **Answer:** 14/3 Ω.  

10. **Maximum and Minimum Resistance from n Resistors:**  
   **Example:** Three resistors (1Ω, 2Ω, 3Ω) are available. Find maximum and minimum resistance.  
   **Method A:** Maximum = Series = 1 + 2 + 3 = 6 Ω; Minimum = Parallel = 1/(1/1 + 1/2 + 1/3) = 6/11 Ω.  
   **Method B:** Direct calculation.  
   **Answer:** Maximum = 6 Ω; Minimum = 6/11 Ω.  

11. **Power of Appliance and Current/Fuse Choice:**  
   **Example:** A 1.5 kW heater is connected to a 230 V supply. Find the current and suggest a fuse rating.  
   **Method A:** I = P/V = 1500/230 ≈ 6.52 A; use a 10 A fuse.  
   **Method B:** Direct calculation.  
   **Answer:** 6.52 A; 10 A fuse.  

12. **Bulbs in Series vs Parallel Brightness:**  
   **Example:** A 60 W bulb and a 100 W bulb are connected in series to a 230 V supply. Which glows brighter?  
   **Method A:** P = V²/R ⇒ R_60 = 230²/60 ≈ 881.67 Ω; R_100 = 230²/100 ≈ 529 Ω. In series, P = I²R ⇒ P_60 > P_100.  
   **Method B:** Higher resistance bulb glows brighter in series.  
   **Answer:** 60 W bulb glows brighter.  

13. **Heat by Joule's Law:**  
   **Example:** A current of 2A flows through a 3Ω resistor for 5s. Find the heat produced.  
   **Method A:** H = I²Rt = (2)^2 × 3 × 5 = 60 J.  
   **Method B:** Direct calculation.  
   **Answer:** 60 J.  

14. **Energy Cost in kWh:**  
   **Example:** A 1.5 kW heater is used for 2 hours daily for 30 days. If electricity costs ₹6/kWh, find the total cost.  
   **Method A:** Energy = 1.5 × 2 × 30 = 90 kWh; Cost = 90 × 6 = ₹540.  
   **Method B:** Direct calculation.  
   **Answer:** ₹540.  

15. **Resistance of a Bulb from Its Rating:**  
   **Example:** A 60 W, 230 V bulb is connected to a 230 V supply. Find its resistance.  
   **Method A:** R = V²/P = 230²/60 ≈ 881.67 Ω.  
   **Method B:** Direct calculation.  
   **Answer:** 881.67 Ω.
