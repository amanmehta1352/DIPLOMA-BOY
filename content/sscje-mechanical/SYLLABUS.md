# SSC JE Mechanical — syllabus map (Nov 2026)

Sources (third-party pages that reproduce the official SSC JE 2026 notice; the SSC page could not be reached, owner allowed this on 31 Oct 2026):
- PW: https://www.pw.live/ae-je/exams/ssc-je-mechanical-engineering-syllabus
- MADE EASY: https://www.madeeasy.in/blog/ssc-je-mechanical-engineering-syllabus
Both list the same 11 technical subjects (Paper-I Part-C has the same headings; Paper-II gives the topics). Level: diploma in mechanical engineering.
Pattern: Paper-I = Reasoning 50 + General Awareness 50 + Mechanical 100 (−0.25); Paper-II = Mechanical 100 Q, 300 marks (−1).

## 11 official subjects  →  app chapters (mtb.topics, subject 6 "Mechanical Engineering")

| # | Official subject | Official topics | App chapter (topic id) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Engineering Mechanics | FBD; equilibrium & resultant; Lami; friction (sliding, rolling); centroid of plane figures; CG of simple solids | Engineering Mechanics (433) |
| 2 | Strength of Materials | stress-strain; types of beams; SF & BM; bending & deflection; torsion; thin cylinders | Strength of Materials (439) |
| 3 | Theory of Machines | cams; belt/chain/rope; gears & trains; flywheel & governor; balancing & vibration; brakes, dynamometers, clutches, bearings | Theory of Machines (440) |
| 4 | Machine Design | loads & stresses; creep & fatigue; failure theories; cotter, knuckle, lever, crank; bearings; key, shaft, coupling; spur gear, power screw, spring; bolts, welds | Machine Design (new) |
| 5 | Material Science & Engg | crystal structure & bonds; Fe-C diagram; heat treatment; cast iron & steels; non-ferrous alloys; (properties, testing, NDT, corrosion) | Engineering Materials (432) |
| 6 | Thermal Engineering | energy sources; laws of TD; power plant, heat engine/pump/refrigerator; boilers, steam turbines, cooling tower; steam properties, nozzle; IC engines & cycles; air compressors; RAC; gas turbine & jet; heat transfer; heat exchangers | Thermodynamics (441), IC Engines (435), RAC (437), Steam Boilers/Turbines/Power Plants (438), Air Compressors (new), Gas Turbine & Jet Propulsion (new), Heat Transfer & Heat Exchangers (new) |
| 7 | Fluid Mechanics & Hydraulic Machinery | fluid properties; fluid flow; pipe flow; continuity; impact of jets; turbines & hydro plants; centrifugal & reciprocating pumps | Fluid Mechanics & Machinery (434) |
| 8 | Industrial Engineering | plant location & layout; work study & measurement; PPC; SQC; inventory | Industrial Engineering (new) |
| 9 | Manufacturing Engineering | pattern, moulding, casting; metal forming; welding; lathes; broaching, drilling, milling; gear making; grinding & finishing; press working | Production Engineering (436) |
| 10 | Metrology & Measurement | precision, accuracy, sensitivity, repeatability, range, calibration; linear & angular measurement; comparators; form & surface finish; CMM; limits, fits, tolerances | Metrology & Measurement (new) |
| 11 | Advanced Manufacturing Processes | jigs & fixtures; CNC & machining centres; PLC; special purpose machines; EDM, ECM, AJM, LBM, EBM; CAD/CAM; robots; FMS | Advanced Manufacturing (new) |
