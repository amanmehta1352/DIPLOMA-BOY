# Physics book: rewrite of chapters 2–11 (plan)

Why: chapters 2–11 come from a source that is not cleared for reuse. Before Play Store or paid courses,
each chapter is rewritten in our own words and structure. Chapter 1 is kept (it is ours).

## Rules for every rewritten chapter
1. No sentence, example, question, figure or table copied from any book. Physics facts stay; wording and examples are new.
2. Same format as now: notes, teach (Hindi/English/Hinglish), formulas, demo, and the 3D tab if the chapter has one.
3. Every number and formula checked by a second pass (recalculate, do not trust the first draft).
4. Written chapter goes to the server as a draft (status = draft), not published, until the owner approves it.
5. Owner reads the draft in the app, then approves. Only then status = published, old chapter is replaced.

## Steps for one chapter
1. Outline: the 5–8 ideas the chapter must teach, in our own order (no source order).
2. Draft notes and teach text from the outline.
3. Write new worked examples (new numbers), and new practice questions.
4. Check: formulas, units, numbers. Mark anything unsure for the owner.
5. Save as draft on the server; owner reviews in the app.
6. Owner approves -> publish; old copy kept in backup schema.

## Done when
- All 10 chapters are drafted, checked and approved by the owner.
- Source file is no longer used anywhere in the live app.
