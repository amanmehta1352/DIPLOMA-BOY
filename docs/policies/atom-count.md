# Atom count policy (proposed)

Status: **approved** (CEO, 1 Nov 2026). Design choices, not official standards.

This expands the depth policy. Per level: total atoms per chapter, what each atom type contains, and difficulty split for MCQs.

| Level | Total | Concept atoms | Formula atoms | Example atoms | MCQ atoms | MCQ difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| class_6_8 | 5 | 2 short, basic definitions | 0 | 1 everyday example | 2 | easy 2 |
| class_9_10 | 10 | 3, one page each | 2, derivation not needed | 2, mix real-life and textbook | 3 | easy 1, medium 2 |
| class_11_12 | 12 | 4, textbook depth | 3, one derivation allowed | 2 | 3 | medium 2, hard 1 |
| NEET / JEE | 15 | 5 | 3, derivation required | 3, abstract and applied | 4 | medium 2, hard 2 |
| SSC / Banking | 10 | 2 | 1, formula sheet style | 2, exam-style | 5 | easy 2, medium 2, hard 1 |
| UPSC | 10 | 4, analytical | 0 | 2, current-affairs style | 4 | medium 2, hard 2 |
| GATE / PhD | 15 | 6, research depth | 4, derivation required | 2, research problems | 3 | hard 3 |

Rules per atom type:
- Concept: one idea per atom, no formulas except those named in the formula atoms.
- Formula: state the formula, define symbols, give units. Derivation only where the table says so.
- Example: worked, with one step explained at a time.
- MCQ: four options (five for SSC/Banking), one correct, distractors based on common mistakes.

Open decision: whether class_6_8 needs any formula atoms.
