### **E. NUMERICAL TYPES**

1. **Find R of Combination Then Current:**  
   **Example:** Three resistors (2Ω, 3Ω, 6Ω) are connected in parallel to a 12 V battery. Find the total current.  
   **Answer:** 1/R = 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/6 = 1; R = 1Ω; I = V/R = 12 A.  

2. **Compare Two Bulbs' Power:**  
   **Example:** Bulb A (60 W, 220 V) and Bulb B (100 W, 220 V) are connected to the same supply. Which glows brighter?  
   **Answer:** Bulb B (100 W) glows brighter.  

3. **Stretched Wire:**  
   **Example:** A wire of length L and resistance R is stretched to 2L. Find its new resistance.  
   **Answer:** R' = 4R (resistance increases with length and decreases with area).  

4. **Cost of Energy:**  
   **Example:** A 1.5 kW heater is used for 2 hours daily for 30 days. If electricity costs ₹5 per unit, find the total cost.  
   **Answer:** Energy = 1.5 kW × 2 h × 30 = 90 kWh; Cost = 90 × 5 = ₹450.

### **F. CBSE QUESTION FORMATS**

1. **MCQ:**  
   **Question:** Which of the following is true for a series circuit?  
   A) Voltage is same across all components.  
   B) Current is same in all components.  
   C) Total resistance decreases.  
   D) Power is inversely proportional to resistance.  
   **Answer:** B) Current is same in all components.  

2. **Assertion-Reason:**  
   **Question:** Assertion: Tungsten is used in filament bulbs.  
   Reason: Tungsten has a high melting point.  
   **Answer:** Both Assertion and Reason are true, and Reason is the correct explanation.  

3. **Case-Based:**  
   **Case:** A household has a 1 kW heater, a 100 W bulb, and a 500 W fan running for 4 hours daily.  
   **Sub-Questions:**  
   1) Calculate the total energy consumed in kWh.  
   2) If the cost per unit is ₹6, find the monthly bill.  
   3) Why are appliances connected in parallel in homes?  
   4) What happens if a fuse rated 10 A is used for this circuit?  
   **Answers:**  
   1) 6.4 kWh  
   2) ₹384  
   3) To ensure each appliance gets full voltage.  
   4) The fuse will trip as the current exceeds 10 A.  

4. **2-Mark:**  
   **Question:** Derive the relation P = I²R.  
   **Answer:** P = VI and V = IR ⇒ P = I(IR) = I²R.  

5. **3-Mark:**  
   **Question:** Explain the heating effect of electric current with two applications.  
   **Answer:** When current flows through a conductor, it heats up due to resistance. Applications: electric heater, incandescent bulb.  

6. **5-Mark:**  
   **Question:** Describe Ohm's law experimentally, including the circuit diagram and observation table.  
   **Answer:** Circuit diagram includes battery, rheostat, ammeter, voltmeter, and resistor. Observation table shows V and I values, with graph confirming linear relationship.
