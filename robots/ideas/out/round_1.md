### Redesigning Each Mode

#### **1. Book (E-book / ई-बुक)**
**Purpose:** Make the e-book feel interactive and engaging, better than a printed textbook on a phone.  
**Screen-by-Screen Experience:**  
- **Screen 1:** Chapter intro with a hook question (e.g., "Why does your phone battery drain faster when you play games?").  
- **Screen 2:** Topic-wise pages with interactive figures (tap to zoom, swipe to navigate). Each topic has a 1-line core idea, a "why" box, and a "common mistake" box.  
- **Screen 3:** Embedded short videos (e.g., "How does a fuse work?").  
- **Screen 4:** Quick quizzes after each topic with instant feedback.  
**Figures Used:** F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6, F7, F8, F9.  
**Student Interaction:** Tap to zoom, swipe to navigate, predict answers before seeing explanations, write short answers in quizzes.

#### **2. Notes (Notebook / नोटबुक)**
**Purpose:** Make notes a student actually revises from, with a handwritten-notebook feel and memory hooks.  
**Screen-by-Screen Experience:**  
- **Screen 1:** One-page summary of the chapter with colour-coded sections (e.g., blue for definitions, green for formulas, red for common mistakes).  
- **Screen 2:** Topic-wise notes with interactive figures (tap to animate).  
- **Screen 3:** Handwritten-style diagrams with labels (students can tap to fill in missing labels).  
- **Screen 4:** Quick revision flashcards with memory hooks (e.g., "Current flows like water in a river").  
**Figures Used:** F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6, F7, F8, F9.  
**Student Interaction:** Tap to animate figures, fill in missing labels, swipe through flashcards.

#### **3. Teach (टीचिंग मोड)**
**Purpose:** A teacher-led lesson with step-by-step reveal and student prediction.  
**Screen-by-Screen Experience:**  
- **Screen 1:** Hook question (e.g., "Why does a bulb glow brighter when connected in parallel?").  
- **Screen 2:** Step-by-step explanation with interactive figures (teacher reveals each step after student predicts).  
- **Screen 3:** Real-life application example (e.g., "Why do we use parallel circuits in homes?").  
- **Screen 4:** Quick recap and MCQ with instant feedback.  
**Figures Used:** F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6, F7, F8, F9.  
**Student Interaction:** Predict answers before seeing explanations, tap to reveal steps, answer MCQs.

#### **4. Formula**
**Purpose:** Beyond a list — formula map, when-to-use, unit traps, derive one from another.  
**Screen-by-Screen Experience:**  
- **Screen 1:** Formula map showing relationships between formulas (e.g., P = VI, P = I²R, P = V²/R).  
- **Screen 2:** When-to-use guide with examples (e.g., "Use P = VI when voltage and current are known").  
- **Screen 3:** Derivation of one formula from another (e.g., derive P = I²R from P = VI).  
- **Screen 4:** Unit traps and common mistakes (e.g., "Don't confuse kW with kWh").  
**Figures Used:** F4, F5, F6, F7, F8.  
**Student Interaction:** Tap to see derivations, drag formulas to match with examples, write correct units.

#### **5. 3D / Lab**
**Purpose:** Simulations and virtual experiments for hands-on learning.  
**Screen-by-Screen Experience:**  
- **Screen 1:** Ohm's law verification experiment (drag sliders for V, I, R to see linear relationship).  
- **Screen 2:** Series vs. parallel circuits with real-time brightness and current measurements.  
- **Screen 3:** Heating effect experiment (change wire material and thickness to see heat produced).  
- **Screen 4:** Home wiring simulation (drag appliances to see MCB tripping).  
**Figures Used:** F1, F4, F6, F7, F9.  
**Student Interaction:** Drag sliders, tap to change materials, predict outcomes before seeing results.

### **Missing Content Items**

1. Definitions of 1 volt, 1 ampere, 1 ohm.
2. Resistivity values table (e.g., copper, nichrome).
3. Why alloys are used in heating elements.
4. Why tungsten is used in bulbs.
5. Fuse working and rating.
6. Derivation of P = I²R = V²/R.
7. Numericals of each type (Ohm's law, series/parallel, heating effect, power).
8. Activity-based questions (e.g., verifying Ohm's law).
9. Assertion-reason and case-based questions (as in CBSE papers).
10. Practical applications of series and parallel circuits (e.g., traffic lights, home wiring).
11. Explanation of MCB and its importance in home wiring.

### **Placement of F9 (Home Wiring and MCB)**

F9 (home wiring and MCB) belongs to **Unit IV: Effects of Current**, specifically under **S5: Parallel combination of resistors and its applications in daily life**. It should be placed in the Teach and 3D-Lab modes to explain the practical application of parallel circuits in home wiring and the role of MCBs.
